Life journey

From discipline to destiny

2002

Early promise in West Delhi

Kohli’s passion for cricket bloomed through relentless practice, local tournaments, and the grounding influence of his family.

2008

Under-19 World Cup win

He led India to the Under-19 World Cup title and announced himself as a future star on the national stage.

2013

Captaincy and dominance

The transition to leadership brought an era of aggressive intent, fearless batting, and unmatched consistency in all formats.

2018

Peak global stature

He became the face of modern Indian cricket with record-breaking runs, iconic knocks, and a culture of excellence.

2023–24

A record-breaking 50th ODI ton, then a world title

He became the first man to 50 ODI centuries at the 2023 World Cup, then closed the chapter on India's T20I trophy drought with the 2024 T20 World Cup win.

2025–26

Farewell to whites, RCB's long-awaited crown

Kohli retired from Test cricket in May 2025, then finally lifted the IPL trophy with RCB — and led them to a second straight title in 2026.

Struggles & strength

Loss, grit, and the rise of a legend

During a Ranji Trophy match, Virat lost his father at a crucial point in his life. That heartbreak shaped his resolve, and he went on to turn adversity into a relentless pursuit of excellence.
